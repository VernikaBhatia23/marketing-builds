# What the best battlecards do (research summary, Nov 2026)

- **One page, 15 seconds.** Reps use cards live. Anything long gets ignored.
- **Know / Say / Show layers.** Facts to know, words to say, proof to show.
- **Landmine questions** plant doubts about the competitor early; **discovery questions** with "Ask this / Listen for" pairs steer toward where we win.
- **When we win / when we lose** stated plainly builds rep trust.
- **Objection handling** as Acknowledge, Praise (briefly), Limit, Return; here simplified to Acknowledge, Reframe, Ask.
- **Proof inside the card**: short case-study lines, quotes, numbers with dates.
- **Freshness**: a "last updated" date per section, one named owner, monthly refresh.
- **Talk tracks and evidence are rare**: one audit found only 43% of cards include talk tracks, 19% include evidence and 35% include proof points. Including all three is the edge.
- Patterns seen in public examples: persona-anchored openers, buyer-fear openers, ultra-scannable comparison tables, inline micro case studies, colour-coded boxes.
