# Research protocol

1. **Open the page.** Search results and snippets are leads, not evidence. Scrape or open the page before quoting numbers.
2. **Log every fact**: `fact | URL | date seen | tag (CR/3P/RV/UV)`.
3. **Cross-check numbers**: headcount, revenue, ratings, prices. If two sources disagree, show the range, tag UV, add to Verify list.
4. **Review sites**: record score, review count, star split, date, and any platform banner (withheld rating, fake-review removal, unclaimed profile, "no invitations").
5. **Quotes**: copy verbatim, note star rating, month and platform. Prefer short, specific lines. If positive quotes are rare for a competitor (even in 1-star reviews), say so on the card.
6. **Customers and logos**: look at the vendor's customer page, press releases and case studies. If no customers are named, show partners/backers named in primary sources only.
7. **Blocked sites**: say they were blocked; do not circumvent.
8. **Stop rule**: when each card section has at least one sourced fact and each of our weaknesses has an honest stance, stop researching and build.
